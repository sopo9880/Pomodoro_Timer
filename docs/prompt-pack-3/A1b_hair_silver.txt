# A1 머리 — hair-silver

첨부할 기준 그림(refs 폴더): front_head.png, front_hair_back.png, side_head.png, side_head_blink.png, side_hair_back.png, back_head.png

## 프롬프트

Cute chibi botanical-fantasy game character art, soft painterly shading with clean thin warm-brown outlines, same style, proportions and face as the attached reference character. Muted natural palette (forest green, cream, warm brown, dusty rose). Flat solid pure magenta background (#FF00FF), no gradient, no floor, no cast shadow, no text, no frame. Every part is a separate piece with a wide magenta gap around it; parts must NOT touch or overlap each other. Draw each part flat and un-posed, at the same scale as the reference part, in the order listed, in a grid, left to right and top to bottom. 
Make the HAIR VARIANT: long silver-white hair with pale blue-grey shadows, same length and wave as the reference. Keep the exact same face, eyes, skin, head shape and small leaf-and-flower hair ornament as the reference; only the hair changes.
Six parts, in this order: (1) front view head WITH face and bangs, (2) front view back-hair piece (the hair hanging behind the body), (3) right-facing side view head with face, (4) the same side head with eyes closed (blink), (5) side view back-hair piece, (6) back view of the head (hair only, no face).
Each part has the same size and framing as its matching reference part so it can be swapped in directly.
