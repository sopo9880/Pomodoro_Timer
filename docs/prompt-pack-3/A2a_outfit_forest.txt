## 프롬프트

Cute chibi botanical-fantasy game character art, soft painterly shading with clean thin warm-brown outlines, same style, proportions and face as the attached reference character. Muted natural palette (forest green, cream, warm brown, dusty rose). Flat solid pure magenta background (#FF00FF), no gradient, no floor, no cast shadow, no text, no frame. Every part is a separate piece with a wide magenta gap around it; parts must NOT touch or overlap each other. Draw each part flat and un-posed, at the same scale as the reference part, in the order listed, in a grid, left to right and top to bottom. 
Make the OUTFIT VARIANT: deep forest-green dress (#305a47) with sage leaf trim and cream lining, small gold leaf buttons. Same cut, length and silhouette as the reference outfit (the skirt must stay short enough that the legs show below it). Only color, trim and embroidery change. Draw clothing only, no body, no head, no hands, no legs.
Eleven parts, in this order: FRONT: (1) torso/bodice, (2) skirt, (3) left sleeve (puff only), (4) right sleeve (puff only). SIDE (right-facing): (5) torso, (6) skirt, (7) NEAR arm, (8) FAR arm, (9) FAR arm for the left-facing walk (the other arm of the pair, different pose from (8)). The three side arms are COMPLETE arms exactly like the attached side_arm_near.png and side_arm_far.png: the outfit's sleeve puff and cuff at the top, with the bare skin forearm and hand hanging below it, all in one piece. BACK: (10) torso, (11) skirt, (12) left sleeve (puff only), (13) right sleeve (puff only).
Match each reference part's size and shape so it can be swapped in directly; front and back sleeves are puffs only (no arm drawn); the side arms are full arms. Do not draw a torso_alt or any other extra piece.
