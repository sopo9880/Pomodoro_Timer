Cute chibi botanical-fantasy game character art, soft painterly shading with clean thin warm-brown outlines, same style, proportions and face as the attached reference character. Every part is a separate piece with a wide magenta gap around it; parts must NOT touch or overlap each other. Draw each part flat and un-posed, at the same scale as the reference part, in the order listed, in a grid, left to right and top to bottom. 
Small ground decorations to hide tiling: 6 wildflower patches, 6 pebble clusters, 6 grass tufts, 6 fallen-leaf piles. 24 pieces, each small, soft, no hard outline.
